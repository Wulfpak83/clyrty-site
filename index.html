FAQ

Good questions, quick answers.

Will Clyrty delete my photos without asking?

No. Swiping only queues a photo. Photos are removed only after you review the queue and approve Apple's delete prompt.

Can I get a photo back?

Before you confirm, restore anything from the queue. After you confirm, items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where they stay for 30 days.

Do I need an account or an upload?

No. There is no sign-up. Clyrty works with the photos on your iPhone and does not upload them.

What do I get for free?

15 removals a day, free. Keeping, hiding, undo and restore stay free, and every pile and tool is open to everyone. Unlimited plans are available inside the app.

Is it on the App Store yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon to the App Store.
