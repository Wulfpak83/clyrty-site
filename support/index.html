I removed a photo by mistake

Open the Photos app and go to Recently Deleted (under Utilities). Apple normally keeps deleted items there for up to 30 days. Select the item and tap Recover.

When do my free removals reset?

You get 15 removals a day, and the count resets at local midnight. Keep, Hide, Later, Undo and Restore are always free.

Restore a purchase

In CLYRTY, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

In CLYRTY, open Settings and tap Manage subscription, or open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal.

Change Photos access

Open the iOS Settings app, find CLYRTY, then Photos. CLYRTY needs full access to sort your whole library.

Turn the daily reminder on or off

In CLYRTY, open Settings and use the Daily reminder switch.

Privacy
