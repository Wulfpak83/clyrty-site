CLYRTY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

CLYRTY works on your iPhone. We do not collect your data: there is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos and your decisions stay on your device.

What CLYRTY uses on your iPhone

  • Photos. With your permission, CLYRTY reads your photo library to sort it into piles, find duplicates, blurry photos and large videos, estimate sizes, and save edited copies you ask for. This happens on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded.
  • Your review history. CLYRTY keeps your decisions (keep, hide, later, queue for removal), the identifiers and dates that Photos assigns to those items, and your progress, in the app's own storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS file protection.
  • Settings. Your reminder time, language, pile order and similar choices are stored on your iPhone.
  • Purchase status. Whether you have an active plan is checked with Apple on your iPhone and remembered there.
  • Home Screen widget. The widget receives numbers only: your streak, how many items are waiting today and the space moved to Recently Deleted this week. It never receives photos, names or identifiers.
  • Reminders. The optional daily reminder is a notification that iOS schedules on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. CLYRTY has no server for your data and does not send your information to us or to anyone else. We do not receive your photos, your review history, your location, your contacts or any identifier from your device.

Deleting photos

CLYRTY never deletes anything on its own. Items you queue are removed only after you confirm Apple's Photos prompt. They then move to Recently Deleted, where Apple normally keeps them for up to 30 days so you can recover them.

Purchases

Payments, subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive your name or your payment details.

When you contact us

If you email support@clyrty.app, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

If you choose Share redacted diagnostics in Settings, you decide where it goes. It contains only the app version, the iOS version, the Photos permission state, your purchase status and the number of items waiting in Pending. It never includes photos or identifiers.

Third parties

CLYRTY contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code from other companies. We do not sell or share personal information. Apple provides the operating system, the App Store and iCloud under its own terms.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove CLYRTY's access to Photos at any time in the iOS Settings app.
  • Deleting CLYRTY removes its data from your iPhone.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include CLYRTY's on-device data in those backups, which you and Apple control.

Children

CLYRTY is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact
