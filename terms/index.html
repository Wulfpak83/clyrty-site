What CLYRTY does

CLYRTY helps you review the photos and videos on your iPhone and decide what to keep. You make every decision. Nothing is deleted until you confirm Apple's Photos prompt; deleted items then move to Recently Deleted, where Apple normally keeps them for up to 30 days. Once Recently Deleted is emptied, by you or by iOS, items cannot be recovered, so please review your choices before you confirm.

Free use

CLYRTY is free to use with up to 15 removals a day. The count resets at local midnight. Keep, Hide, Later, Undo and Restore are always free.

Plans

  • Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited removals.
